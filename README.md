# Asha Study: download

Asha Study is a calm study companion for [LearnHarness](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness) on Windows. It adds a garden that grows as you study, gentle streaks, a time jar and a study calendar.

Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Download

Get **Asha-Study-0.3.0.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

## Install (about 5 minutes)

You need Windows 10 or 11 with LearnHarness 0.6 or newer installed.

1. Unzip the download and open the **Asha Study** folder.
2. Follow **Start here.txt**. In short:
   - Run **Asha-Study-Companion-Setup.exe**. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. No administrator password is needed.
   - Unzip **Plugins\asha-study-0.5.0.zip** and copy the **asha-study** folder into `%APPDATA%\LearnHarness\data\plugins`.
   - In LearnHarness, open **Settings > Community plugins** and turn **Asha Study** on.
3. A **Garden** button appears in the LearnHarness sidebar, under Practice.

To uninstall, remove **Asha Study Companion** in Windows Settings > Apps, and delete the `asha-study` folder from the LearnHarness plugins folder.
